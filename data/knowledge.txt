# FICTIONAL TEST DATA — NOT A REAL GOVERNMENT SCHEME

**Dataset:** EduAssist Fictional Grant Program (EFGP)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Testing a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline  
**Status:** Entirely fictional. Not valid government guidance.

This document describes an imaginary educational assistance program in the fictional nation of Exemplia. The program, currency, eligibility criteria, benefits, documents, portal, authorities, deadlines, and contact details are invented for software testing. They must never be interpreted as actual government policy.

The fictional currency used throughout this dataset is the Fictional Currency Unit (FCU).

# 1. SCHEME OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Official fictional name

The fictional program is called the EduAssist Fictional Grant Program, abbreviated as EFGP. Its full dataset designation is “EduAssist Fictional Grant Program (EFGP) — Test Dataset v1.0.”

## 1.2 Purpose

EFGP is a hypothetical financial assistance initiative designed to support students from economically disadvantaged households who are pursuing higher education or recognized vocational training.

Its fictional objectives are to reduce student dropout rates, ease tuition-related financial pressure, support access to study materials, assist with living expenses, and encourage continued academic participation.

The program combines tuition assistance, a monthly living stipend, a study material allowance, examination fee reimbursement, mentorship access, and an internet subsidy.

## 1.3 Intended beneficiaries

The fictional program is intended for eligible students enrolled in either:
- An accredited undergraduate degree program.
- A government-recognized vocational training course.

Applicants must also satisfy the age, household income, citizenship, academic standing, and other eligibility conditions defined in this dataset.

## 1.4 Important exclusions

EFGP is fictional and has no real application portal or administering authority.

The program does not provide laptops, smartphones, tablets, or other physical hardware. Students are responsible for obtaining any hardware they need for their studies.

The dataset does not establish a real-world legal entitlement, funding allocation, application opportunity, or payment guarantee.

# 2. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS

## 2.1 General eligibility rule

An applicant must meet all mandatory eligibility conditions to qualify for the fictional EFGP grant. Meeting only some conditions does not establish eligibility.

The first-generation learner preference is a prioritization factor, not a mandatory eligibility condition.

## 2.2 Age requirement

An applicant must be between 17 and 30 years old at the time of application submission.

For this dataset, the age range is inclusive: an applicant aged 17 or 30 satisfies the stated age criterion. An applicant younger than 17 or older than 30 does not satisfy it.

The dataset does not specify special age relaxations, exceptions, or alternative age calculation rules.

## 2.3 Enrollment requirement

The applicant must currently be enrolled in one of the following:
1. An accredited undergraduate degree program.
2. A government-recognized vocational training course.

The dataset does not establish eligibility for school-level education, postgraduate programs, doctoral studies, informal training, or unrecognized courses. These cases should be treated as unspecified rather than automatically approved or rejected.

Proof of enrollment must be provided through an official institutional letter or certificate that confirms current enrollment and course details.

## 2.4 Household income requirement

The combined annual gross income of the applicant's household must not exceed 250,000 FCU.

The maximum permitted income is inclusive. A household income of exactly 250,000 FCU satisfies the stated income condition. An income greater than 250,000 FCU does not.

The threshold concerns combined annual gross household income, not merely the applicant's personal earnings.

The dataset does not define which household members must be counted, how irregular income should be calculated, or how income in a different currency should be converted. Those situations require additional rules that are not present in this test dataset.

An income certificate issued within the previous six months is required as evidence.

## 2.5 Citizenship requirement

The applicant must be a citizen of the fictional nation of Exemplia.

Residence in Exemplia alone is not stated to be sufficient. The dataset does not establish eligibility for foreign citizens, permanent residents, temporary residents, or dual citizens.

A citizenship document or passport issued by the fictional government of Exemplia is listed as acceptable citizenship evidence.

## 2.6 Academic standing requirement

The applicant must have passed the preceding academic year, or an equivalent academic period, with a minimum grade of 50 percent.

A grade of exactly 50 percent satisfies this condition. A grade below 50 percent does not satisfy the stated academic threshold.

The dataset does not define alternative grading systems, grade-point conversions, failed subjects with supplementary examinations, or special treatment for students in their first academic year.

Applicants must submit mark sheets or grade reports for their most recently completed academic year.

## 2.7 Concurrent benefit restriction

The applicant must not be receiving an equivalent education grant from another fictional program during the same period.

The restriction concerns an equivalent education grant, not every form of financial support a student might receive. However, this dataset does not define which other grants count as equivalent.

Applicants must submit a self-declaration stating that they are not receiving a concurrent grant from another scheme.

The dataset does not specify the penalty, appeal process, or recovery procedure if an applicant is later found to have received an equivalent concurrent grant.

## 2.8 First-generation learner preference

Applicants who are the first people in their immediate family to pursue higher education receive priority consideration.

This preference is not a strict eligibility requirement. An applicant who is not a first-generation learner may still qualify if all mandatory eligibility conditions are met.

The dataset does not define the exact meaning of immediate family, the evidence needed to establish first-generation status, or the number of priority points awarded.

Priority consideration must not be represented as a guarantee of approval.

# 3. BENEFITS AND FINANCIAL LIMITS

## 3.1 Tuition fee waiver

The fictional program covers up to 80 percent of annual tuition fees, subject to a maximum of 40,000 FCU per academic year.

The amount is constrained by both the percentage limit and the annual cap.

For example:
- If annual tuition fees are 20,000 FCU, 80 percent is 16,000 FCU. The maximum calculated assistance is 16,000 FCU.
- If annual tuition fees are 50,000 FCU, 80 percent is 40,000 FCU. The maximum calculated assistance is 40,000 FCU.
- If annual tuition fees are 60,000 FCU, 80 percent is 48,000 FCU, but the annual cap limits assistance to 40,000 FCU.

The tuition benefit can be expressed as:

Tuition assistance = the smaller of 80% of annual tuition fees or 40,000 FCU.

The dataset does not state whether any remaining tuition balance can be covered through other EFGP benefits.

Tuition fee payments are made directly to the educational institution on behalf of the approved beneficiary.

## 3.2 Monthly living stipend

An approved beneficiary receives a fictional monthly living allowance of 3,000 FCU.

The stipend is disbursed directly to the student's registered bank account.

If paid for 12 months, the nominal annual total would be 36,000 FCU. However, the dataset does not specify the precise annual payment calendar, whether every beneficiary receives 12 payments, or whether payments are suspended during academic breaks.

Do not assume a guaranteed annual total unless the payment period is established.

## 3.3 Study material allowance

The program provides a one-time annual study material allowance of 5,000 FCU.

The allowance is intended for educational materials such as textbooks, laboratory supplies, and digital learning subscriptions.

The dataset describes this benefit as an annual grant. It does not specify reimbursement procedures, required receipts, permitted vendors, or whether unused funds can be carried forward.

## 3.4 Examination fee reimbursement

The program fully reimburses fees for up to two national or university examinations per academic year.

The stated limit is two examinations per academic year. The dataset does not establish reimbursement for a third examination, entrance coaching, private tutoring, late fees, travel expenses, or unrelated certification examinations.

The exact submission process for examination fee receipts is not specified.

## 3.5 Mentorship access

Approved beneficiaries receive access to the fictional EFGP Mentorship Network.

The network connects beneficiaries with voluntary academic mentors. Its stated purpose is to provide academic guidance and support.

The dataset does not promise a particular mentor, a minimum number of sessions, job placement, internship placement, or guaranteed academic improvement.

## 3.6 Internet subsidy

The program provides a fictional monthly broadband subsidy of 500 FCU to support online learning.

The dataset does not specify eligible internet service providers, whether mobile data is covered, what receipts are required, or whether the subsidy can be combined with another internet benefit.

## 3.7 Hardware exclusion

EFGP does not provide laptops, smartphones, tablets, computers, or other physical hardware devices.

The student is responsible for procuring any hardware needed for educational activities.

The study material allowance and internet subsidy must not be interpreted as a promise of a free laptop or smartphone.

If an applicant asks whether EFGP supplies a laptop, the correct answer from this dataset is no.

## 5.1 Step 1 — Register on the portal

The applicant visits the fictional portal, efgp.exemplia.test, and creates an account using a valid email address.

The ARN is used to identify the applicant during subsequent steps in the fictional process.

## 5.2 Step 2 — Complete the application form

The applicant logs in using the ARN and completes Form EFGP-2024-A online.

The form requires personal, academic, and financial information.

The applicant is expected to provide accurate information. The dataset does not specify an edit-after-submission procedure.

## 5.3 Step 3 — Upload documents

The applicant uploads scanned or photographed copies of all required documents.

Each file must be in PDF or JPEG format and must not exceed 2 MB.

Applicants should ensure that documents are readable and contain the information required for verification.

The dataset does not define a separate document resubmission workflow for illegible files.

## 5.4 Step 4 — Submit and acknowledge

After completing the form and uploading the documents, the applicant reviews the submission and selects Submit.

An acknowledgement email is then described as being sent to the registered email address. It contains a submission timestamp and confirmation code.

An acknowledgement confirms receipt in the fictional process. It is not the same as a grant approval.

## 5.5 Step 5 — Verification

The fictional EFGP Secretariat reviews submitted applications within 30 working days.

During verification, applicants may be contacted for additional information or document verification.

The dataset does not define the official working-day calendar, public holiday treatment, or whether the review period pauses while an applicant supplies additional information.

## 5.6 Step 6 — Decision notification

Applicants are notified of the decision through email and the fictional portal.

Approved applicants receive a Grant Approval Letter specifying the benefit components and disbursement schedule.

The dataset does not define a detailed rejection notice template or a formal appeal deadline.

## 5.7 Step 7 — Disbursement

After a Grant Approval Letter is issued, the monthly stipend and study material allowance are described as being disbursed directly to the registered bank account.

Tuition fee payments are made directly to the institution on the beneficiary's behalf.

The dataset does not define the exact date of the first payment, the payment schedule for examination reimbursements, or the payment mechanism for the internet subsidy.

## 5.8 Step 8 — Annual renewal

The fictional grant is renewable annually.

To renew, the beneficiary must submit:
- An updated income certificate.
- Current proof of enrollment.
- Academic transcripts showing satisfactory progress.

The documents must be submitted before the renewal deadline.

The exact renewal deadline, renewal portal procedure, and definition of satisfactory progress are not specified in this dataset.
