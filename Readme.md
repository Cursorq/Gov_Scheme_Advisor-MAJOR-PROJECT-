## 📖 Abstract
Government welfare schemes provide crucial support to citizens, but finding the right one is often overwhelming. Our system uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to let users describe their situation in plain text and receive highly relevant, source-grounded scheme recommendations instantly.
